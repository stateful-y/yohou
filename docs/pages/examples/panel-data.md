# Panel Data

Yohou handles multi-series panel datasets natively. These notebooks cover every aspect of working with panel data: per-column-subset models with [`ColumnForecaster`](/pages/api/generated/yohou.compose.column_forecaster.ColumnForecaster/), per-series models with [`LocalPanelForecaster`](/pages/api/generated/yohou.compose.local_panel_forecaster.LocalPanelForecaster/), panel preprocessing and stationarity, prediction intervals across groups, and panel cross-validation.
